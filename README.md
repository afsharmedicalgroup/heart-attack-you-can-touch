# The Heart Attack You Can Touch

A procedural, interactive 3D educational heart visualization from a Gemini 4 Argon creative-engineering challenge, reviewed and corrected by a physician.

## Interact
- Normal coronary flow in a beating schematic heart
- Simulated proximal LAD occlusion and anterior-ischemia changes
- Simplified PCI reperfusion and a conceptual ischemic-injury risk continuum
- 25-second guided demonstration

## Scientific limitations
This is an educational visualization, not a patient-specific anatomical reconstruction, diagnostic ECG, or validated fluid/biomechanical model. The qualitative risk slider is not an infarct-size calculator and makes no time-to-necrosis predictions. Cardiac anatomy, ECG traces, contractility, flow, and PCI are simplified illustrative representations.

## Requirements
WebGL-capable browser with internet access (Three.js, Tailwind CSS, and fonts loaded from public CDNs). No proprietary assets, medical records, or patient information.
